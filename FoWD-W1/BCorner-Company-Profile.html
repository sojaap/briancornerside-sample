About Us

Built From The Streets, Built For Speed

Brian's Corner was founded with one mission: give every driver the car they've always dreamed of. Starting as a small shop in downtown Miami, we've grown into one of the most respected custom garages on the East Coast.

From full body kits and wide-body conversions to engine swaps and nitrous systems, our team of certified mechanics and fabricators bring your vision to life — no compromises, no shortcuts.
